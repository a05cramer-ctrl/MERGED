    5d40b8arules: publish the limits

    The rules it runs on.

    Published before launch. These are launch targets.

    .github/AGENT_RULES.mdlaunch targets
    1. # What MERGED works on
    2. -Open issues on repos with 1,000+ stars, labelled bug, good first issue or help wanted.
    4. # How it behaves
    5. -Says it's an AI in every PR. First line, no fine print.
    6. -Skips any repo whose CONTRIBUTING file or AI policy says no AI PRs.
    7. -Max 1 open PR per repo.
    8. -Max 10 PRs a day, across all repos.
    9. -No drive-by PRs. No typo fixes, no reformatting.
    10. -Closes its own PR if a maintainer asks. No argument.
    12. # What counts
    13. -A PR counts when a maintainer merges it. Opened doesn't count.
    14. <!-- closed and rejected PRs stay on the receipts board -->
